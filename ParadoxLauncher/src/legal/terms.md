## 6. Paradox realms

A Paradox realm is run on its host's own PC: the host's computer runs the realm's Ramsgate and hunts, and everyone in the
realm connects to it. Progress made in a realm is saved to your Mystic Paradox account, so these terms apply there too.

- **Only join realms and invite players you trust.** Mystic Paradox is open source, so anyone can study how it works,
  and a player or host with bad intent may try to disrupt your game or exploit bugs in the game. Keep Windows and your
  antivirus up to date, and report abuse to us.
- Realm traffic goes through a relay by default, so other players see a relay address, not your IP address.
- A host may kick or ban players from their realm. Hosts must not use that, or their PC's role, to harm players.
- We may check what a realm's server saved and undo rewards gained by cheating or exploits, for every player involved.
